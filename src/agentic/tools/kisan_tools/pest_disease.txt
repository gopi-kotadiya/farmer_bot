=== GEHU (WHEAT) KE KEEDE AUR BIMARI ===
================================================================

BIMARIYAN:

1. YELLOW RUST (PEELI KINK):
   Pehchaan: Patto par peeli dhariyan - powder jesi
   Kab: Thand mein - November se January
   Ilaj: Propiconazole 25 EC - 1 ml per liter paani spray
   Bachav: Resistant variety lagayein (HD-2967, PBW-343)

2. BROWN RUST (BHOORI KINK):
   Pehchaan: Patto par bhoore-orange rang ke pustules
   Ilaj: Propiconazole spray karein turant
   Samay: February-March mein zyada

3. BLACK RUST (STEM RUST):
   Pehchaan: Tane aur patto par kale pustules
   Ilaj: Mancozeb 75 WP - 2.5 gram per liter spray
   Khaas: Agar zyada ho to do spray 10 din ke antral par

4. KARNAL BUNT:
   Pehchaan: Dane andar se kale, gande smell
   Bachav: Vitavax 75 WP se beej upchar (2.5 gram per kg beej)
   Certified seed hi lagayein

5. LOOSE SMUT:
   Pehchaan: Bali mein daane nahi - sirf kaala powder
   Bachav: Hot water treatment (52 degree Celsius 10 min) ya Carboxin
   Note: Ek baar ho gaya to koi ilaj nahi - prevention important

6. POWDERY MILDEW:
   Pehchaan: Patto par safed powder jesi parat
   Ilaj: Triadimefon 25 WP - 1 gram per liter spray

KEEDE:

1. APHID (MAAHU):
   Pehchaan: Chote hare-peele keede patto ke neeche
   Nuksan: Ras choste hain - patte curl ho jaate hain
   Ilaj: Imidacloprid 17.8 SL - 250 ml per hectare
   Ya: Thiamethoxam 25 WG - 100 gram per hectare
   Biological: Syrphid flies aur ladybird beetles natural enemies

2. TERMITE (DEEMAK):
   Pehchaan: Mitti mein tunnel banate hain, roots khaate hain
   Paudha ek taraf gir ke sookh jata hai
   Ilaj: Chlorpyriphos 20 EC - 2.5 liter per hectare - mitti mein
   Bachav: FYM acchi tarah paka ke dein

3. ARMY WORM:
   Pehchaan: Raaton mein patte khaate hain - subah mitti mein milte hain
   Ilaj: Chlorpyriphos spray karein - shaam ko

=== DHAN (RICE) KE KEEDE AUR BIMARI ===
================================================================

BIMARIYAN:

1. BLAST BIMARI (SABSE KHATARNAK):
   Pehchaan: Patto par diamond shape bhoore dabbe - white center
   Neck blast: Bali ka tana kala ho ke toot jata hai
   Ilaj: Tricyclazole 75 WP - 6 gram per 15 liter paani
   Ya: Isoprothiolane 40 EC - 1.5 ml per liter
   Samay: Monsoon mein temperature 25-28 degree ho to zyada
   Khaas: Pehli spray symptoms dekh ke, doosri 10 din baad

2. SHEATH BLIGHT:
   Pehchaan: Paani ki line ke paas tane par oval dabbe
   Pehle safed phir bhoore ho jaate hain
   Ilaj: Hexaconazole 5 SC - 2 ml per liter spray
   Ya: Validamycin 3L - 2 ml per liter spray

3. BACTERIAL LEAF BLIGHT (BLB):
   Pehchaan: Patte ki edge se peela-orange rang
   Kaafi hawa aur baarish ke baad zyada
   Ilaj: Copper Oxychloride spray karein
   Resistant variety lagayein (IR-36, Swarna Sub-1)

4. FALSE SMUT:
   Pehchaan: Bali mein kuch dane orange-green powder se dhake
   Ilaj: Propiconazole spray karein heading se pehle

5. BROWN SPOT:
   Pehchaan: Patto par bhoore oval dabbe - yellow halo ke saath
   Poshan ki kami mein zyada hota hai
   Ilaj: Mancozeb spray + Zinc dein mitti mein

KEEDE:

1. BROWN PLANT HOPPER (BPH):
   Pehchaan: Gol brown keede - paani ke paas - paudhe neeche sukh rahe
   "Hopperburn" - circular sukha patches khet mein
   Ilaj: Imidacloprid 17.8 SL ya BPMC spray karein
   Bachav: Resistant variety (MTU-1010), zyada Nitrogen mat dein

2. STEM BORER (TANA CHHEDAK):
   Pehchaan: Dead heart - paudhe ka centre sukh jata hai
   White ear - bali andar se khaali
   Ilaj: Chlorpyriphos granules - 10 kg per acre mitti mein
   Ya: Cartap Hydrochloride spray karein
   Samay: 30-35 din ke paudhe par attack karta hai

3. GALL MIDGE:
   Pehchaan: Tana fool jaata hai - silver shoot banta hai
   Ilaj: Carbofuran 3G - 33 kg per hectare mitti mein
   Bachav: Resistant variety lagayein

4. LEAF FOLDER:
   Pehchaan: Patte fold ho jaate hain - andar keeda
   Ilaj: Chlorpyriphos spray karein

5. HISPA:
   Pehchaan: Patto par safed dhariyan - keeda patta khaata hai
   Ilaj: Acephate spray karein

=== TOMATO (TAMATAR) KE KEEDE AUR BIMARI ===
================================================================

BIMARIYAN:

1. EARLY BLIGHT (ALTERNARIA):
   Pehchaan: Purane patto par bhoore dabbe - target board pattern
   Yellow halo ke saath circles mein dabbe
   Ilaj: Mancozeb 75 WP - 2 gram per liter spray
   Ya: Chlorothalonil spray karein
   10 din ke antral par spray karein

2. LATE BLIGHT (PHYTOPHTHORA):
   Pehchaan: Patte aur tane par dark green to black dabbe
   Neeche safed fungal growth dikhti hai
   Fruits bhi sada jaate hain
   Ilaj: Metalaxyl + Mancozeb (Ridomil Gold) - 2 gram per liter
   Khaas: Bahut jaldi failti hai - turant spray karein

3. FUSARIUM WILT:
   Pehchaan: Ek taraf patte murjha jaate hain - tane andar brown
   Ilaj: Trichoderma viride soil application karein
   Carbendazim drench karein
   Resistant variety use karein

4. BACTERIAL WILT:
   Pehchaan: Paudha achanak mur jata hai - tane se safed pani aata hai
   Ilaj: Koi chemical nahi - infected paudhe nikaalo
   Crop rotation zaroor karein
   Raised bed mein ugayein

5. TOMATO MOSAIC VIRUS (ToMV):
   Pehchaan: Patte mottled (chita) aur curl ho jaate hain
   Fruits chote aur misshapen
   Ilaj: Aphid aur whitefly control karein (vector hain)
   Infected paudhe nikaal dein

6. LEAF CURL VIRUS:
   Pehchaan: Patte upar ki taraf curl ho jaate hain - chhote
   Ilaj: Whitefly control karein - virus failata hai

KEEDE:

1. FRUIT BORER (HELICOVERPA):
   Sabse bada dushman tomato ka
   Pehchaan: Fruit mein gol chhed - andar caterpillar
   Ilaj: Spinosad 45 SC - 0.3 ml per liter spray
   Ya: Emamectin Benzoate 5 SG - 0.5 gram per liter
   Biological: NPV (Nuclear Polyhedrosis Virus) spray karein

2. WHITEFLY:
   Pehchaan: Chote safed keede - patte hilane par ud jaate hain
   Virus failate hain - bahut dangerous
   Ilaj: Imidacloprid 17.8 SL - 0.5 ml per liter
   Ya: Thiamethoxam spray karein
   Yellow sticky traps lagayein

3. APHID (MAAHU):
   Pehchaan: Patto ke neeche chote hare-kale keede
   Ras choste hain + virus failate hain
   Ilaj: Dimethoate 30 EC - 1.5 ml per liter spray

4. SPIDER MITE:
   Pehchaan: Bahut chote - patto ke neeche web (jala) banate hain
   Patte bronze ya silver rang ke ho jaate hain
   Garmi mein zyada
   Ilaj: Propargite spray karein
   Ya: Abamectin 1.9 EC spray

5. THRIPS:
   Pehchaan: Bahut chote - patte silver rang mein
   Pallo ki nasal ke paas dhamke
   Ilaj: Spinosad spray karein

   Pehchaan: Boll mein chhed - green caterpillar
   Ilaj: Emamectin Benzoate spray
   Biological: HaNPV spray karein

3. WHITEFLY:
   Sabse zyada problem aaj kal - CLCuV virus failata hai
   Pehchaan: Patto ke neeche safed keede
   Ilaj: Thiamethoxam 25 WG - 100 gram per hectare
   Imidacloprid spray karein

4. APHID:
   Ilaj: Dimethoate spray karein

5. JASSID (LEAFHOPPER):
   Pehchaan: Patte kinare se peele ho jaate hain - cup shaped
   Ilaj: Imidacloprid spray karein

6. THRIPS:
   Pehchaan: Patte silver rang - thrips andar
   Ilaj: Spinosad spray karein

7. MEALYBUG:
   Pehchaan: Safed powder jaisi insects - bunches mein

=== INTEGRATED PEST MANAGEMENT (IPM) ===
================================================================

IPM KE MUKHYA SIDDHANT:

1. FASAL CHAKKAR (CROP ROTATION):
   - Ek hi fasal baar baar mat lagao
   - Pest ka cycle toot ta hai
   - Dal fasal ke baad anaj fasal

2. RESISTANT VARIETIES:
   - Wherever available - use karein
   - Seed companies se certified seed lo
   - KVK se advice lo

3. CULTURAL PRACTICES:
   - Deep plowing garmi mein - keede marte hain
   - Proper drainage - fungal disease kam
   - Timely sowing - pest escape ho jata hai
   - Balanced fertilizers dein - zyada Nitrogen mat dein

4. BIOLOGICAL CONTROL:
   - Natural enemies ko bachao - Lady beetle, Syrphid fly
   - Bt (Bacillus thuringiensis) spray - caterpillars ke liye
   - Trichoderma - soil borne diseases ke liye
   - Trichogramma cards lagao - egg parasite

5. MECHANICAL METHODS:
   - Yellow sticky traps - whitefly, thrips ke liye
   - Pheromone traps - moths ke liye (Helicoverpa, Spodoptera)
   - Light traps - raaton ke moths ke liye
   - Hand picking - bade keede aur eggs

6. CHEMICAL CONTROL (ANTIM UPAAYA):
   - Tab spray karein jab aur koi option na ho
   - Economic threshold level (ETL) ke baad hi spray
   - Label padein aur dose follow karein
   - Safety equipment pahno - gloves, mask
   - Spray ke 7-14 din baad khaana mat todon

=== SPRAY KE NIYAM (SPRAY RULES) ===
================================================================

SPRAY KARNE KA SAHI TARIKA:
1. Subah ya shaam spray karein - garmi mein nahi
2. Hawa na chal rahi ho to spray karein
3. Baarish hone se pehle spray mat karein
4. Mixing sahi karein - label padein
5. Nozzle se uniform spray karein
6. Patto ke neeche bhi spray karein - keede wahan hote hain

SAFETY:
- Mask, gloves, full sleeves kapde pahno
- Spray karte waqt cigarette, khaana, paani mat lo
- Spray ke baad haath dholo, kapde badlo
- Dawayi andar lene ka - turant doctor ke paas jaao
- Bachon se door rakhein chemicals

MIXING ORDER:
1. Pehle paani lo
2. Fir wettable powder (WP) milao - pehle thoda paani mein gholo
3. Fir EC ya liquid milao
4. Mix karein achhi tarah
5. Sprayer mein daalo

DOSAGE YAAD RAKHEIN:
- Label mein jo likha hai wohi follow karein
- Zyada dose = zyada nuksan (crop burn, resistance)
- Kam dose = keeda nahi marega (resistance banta hai)
- Sahi dose = sahi result

WAITING PERIOD (SPRAY KE BAAD KHATNA):
- Fruits ya vegetables spray ke kitne din baad todin:
  * Imidacloprid: 7 din wait karein
  * Chlorpyriphos: 14 din wait karein
  * Spinosad: 3 din wait karein
  * Mancozeb: 7 din wait karein

=== JAIVIK (ORGANIC) KEEDE CONTROL ===
================================================================

NEEM OIL SPRAY:
- 5 ml neem oil per liter paani
- 1 gram detergent soap milao (emulsifier)
- Ache se hilao phir spray
- Har 10-15 din mein repeat karein
- Soft bodied insects ke liye best (Aphid, Whitefly, Mite)

GARLIC SPRAY:
- 100 gram lehsun pees lo
- 1 liter paani mein 24 ghante bhigowo
- Filter karein + 10 liter paani mein milao
- Spray karein
- Aphids, caterpillars ke liye

CHILI-GARLIC SPRAY:
- 250 gram mirchi + 250 gram lehsun pees lo
- 4 liter paani mein ubaalein
- Thanda karein, filter karein
- 10 liter paani mein milao + soap
- General insecticide

TOBACCO SPRAY:
- 1 kg tobacco powder
- 10 liter paani mein 24 ghante bhigowo
- Filter karein, 50 liter paani mein milao
- Sucking insects ke liye effective

DASHAPARNI ARK:
- 10 alag alag patte pees lo
- Neem, Dhatura, Papaya, Tulsi, Castor wagera
- 10 liter paani mein boil karein
- Filter karein - 3% spray karein
- Broad spectrum organic pesticide
