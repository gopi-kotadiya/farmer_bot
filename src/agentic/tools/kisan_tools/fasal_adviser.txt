=== GEHU (WHEAT) - RABI FASAL ===
================================================================

PARICHAY:
Gehu India ki sabse important Rabi fasal hai. Yeh November-December mein boya jata hai aur March-April mein kata jata hai. India mein sabse zyada gehu Punjab, Haryana, Uttar Pradesh, Madhya Pradesh aur Rajasthan mein hota hai.

BONA KA SAMAY:
- Samay: October ke antim hafte se December ke pehle hafte tak
- Best samay: November 1 se November 20 tak
- Der se bona: December mein bona ho sakta hai par yield kam hogi
- Beej dar: 100-125 kg per hectare (seedbed mein boya jaye)

MITTI AUR KHET KI TAIYARI:
- Mitti: Domat mitti (loamy soil) sabse achhi hoti hai
- pH: 6.0 se 7.5 ke beech hona chahiye
- Khet 2-3 baar jotna chahiye
- Pehli jotai mitti palatne wale haal se karein
- Aakhri jotai mein mitti bilkul barik karein
- Khet bilkul seedha aur samtal hona chahiye

KHAD (FERTILIZER):
- DAP (Di-Ammonium Phosphate): 50 kg per hectare - bote samay
- Urea: 130 kg per hectare - teen baar mein dein
  * Pehli baar: Bote samay 45 kg
  * Doosri baar: Pehli sinchai ke baad 45 kg
  * Teesri baar: Doosri sinchai ke baad 40 kg
- Potash: 50 kg per hectare - bote samay
- Zinc Sulphate: 25 kg per hectare - 3 saal mein ek baar

SINCHAI (IRRIGATION):
- Kul sinchai: 4-6 baar
- Pehli sinchai: Bote ke 20-25 din baad (Crown Root Initiation stage)
- Doosri sinchai: 40-45 din baad (Tillering stage)
- Teesri sinchai: 60-65 din baad (Jointing stage)
- Chauthi sinchai: 80-85 din baad (Flowering stage)
- Paanchvi sinchai: 100-105 din baad (Grain filling stage)
- Note: Pehli sinchai sabse zaroori hai - kabhi mat chukein

BEEJ KI UNNAT KISAMEN (VARIETIES):
- HD-2967: Sabse popular, zyada paidawar
- HD-3086: Gehu blast resistant
- PBW-343: Punjab ke liye best
- K-307: UP ke liye best
- GW-322: Gujarat ke liye best
- Lok-1: Madhya Pradesh ke liye

AAMDANI (YIELD):
- Average: 40-50 quintal per hectare
- Achhe management se: 55-65 quintal per hectare

BIMARI AUR ILAJ:
1. Gehu ka Rust (Kink):
   - Pehchaan: Patto par orange ya brown rang ke dabbe
   - Ilaj: Propiconazole 25 EC - 1 ml per liter paani mein spray karein
   
2. Karnal Bunt:
   - Pehchaan: Dane kale ho jaate hain, gande smell aati hai
   - Bachav: Beej ko Carboxin se upchar karein bote se pehle
   
3. Loose Smut:
   - Pehchaan: Bali mein daane ki jagah kaala powder
   - Ilaj: Vitavax 75 WP se beej upchar karein

KEEDE (INSECTS):
1. Aphid (Maahu):
   - Pehchaan: Chote hare ya kale keede patto par chipke hote hain
   - Ilaj: Imidacloprid 17.8 SL - 250 ml per hectare spray karein
   
2. Termite (Deemak):
   - Ilaj: Chlorpyriphos 20 EC - 2.5 liter per hectare mitti mein milayein

KATAI (HARVESTING):
- Samay: March se April ke beech
- Pehchaan: Jab dane sakht ho jayein aur pila rang aa jaye
- Machine se katai best hai
- Kate ke turant threshing karein

=== DHAN (PADDY/RICE) - KHARIF FASAL ===
================================================================

PARICHAY:
Dhan India ki sabse important Kharif fasal hai. Yeh June-July mein boya jata hai aur October-November mein kata jata hai. India mein sabse zyada dhan West Bengal, UP, Punjab, Andhra Pradesh, aur Tamil Nadu mein hota hai.

BONA KA SAMAY:
- Nursery: May ke antim se June ke pehle hafte mein
- Ropai (Transplanting): June ke antim se July tak
- Beej dar nursery mein: 25-30 kg per hectare
- Nursery area: Ek hectare ke liye 800-1000 sq meter nursery kaafi hai

NURSERY TAIYAR KARNA:
- Beej ko 24 ghante paani mein bhigoyein
- Phir nami kapdey mein 2 din rakhein ankur aane tak
- Nursery mein 2-3 cm gehri seedbeds banayein
- Beej barayein aur paani bharein
- 25-30 din mein paudhe ropai ke liye taiyar

MITTI AUR KHET KI TAIYARI:
- Mitti: Chiknai wali mitti (clayey soil) sabse best
- pH: 5.5 se 6.5
- Khet mein 10-15 cm paani bharo phir jotai karo (pudding)
- Khet bilkul samtal karo - paani ek samaan bharne ke liye

KHAD (FERTILIZER):
- Nitrogen (Urea): 120 kg per hectare
  * Pehli baar: Ropai ke 1 hafte baad - 40 kg
  * Doosri baar: Tillering stage - 40 kg
  * Teesri baar: Panicle initiation - 40 kg
- DAP: 60 kg per hectare - ropai se pehle
- Potash (MOP): 40 kg per hectare - ropai se pehle
- Zinc Sulphate: 25 kg per hectare har 2-3 saal mein

SINCHAI (IRRIGATION):
- Ropai ke baad 5-7 cm paani rakhein lagatar
- Tillering stage mein 2-3 cm paani kaafi hai
- Flower aane se pehle aur baad mein paani zaroor rakhein
- Pakne se 2 hafte pehle paani band karein

UNNAT KISAMEN (VARIETIES):
- Pusa Basmati 1121: Basmati - export quality
- PR-126: Punjab ke liye - kam paani mein
- MTU-1010: Andhra Pradesh ke liye
- Swarna (MTU-7029): Jharkhand, Odisha ke liye
- IR-36: Kide se bachne wali
- HKR-127: Haryana ke liye

BIMARI AUR ILAJ:
1. Blast Bimari:
   - Pehchaan: Patton par diamond shape ke bhoore dabbe
   - Ilaj: Tricyclazole 75 WP - 6 gram per 15 liter paani spray
   
2. Sheath Blight:
   - Pehchaan: Tane par pani mein bhoore dabbe
   - Ilaj: Hexaconazole 5 SC - 2 ml per liter paani spray
   
3. Brown Plant Hopper (BPH):
   - Pehchaan: Paudhe neeche se sukh rahe hain
   - Ilaj: Imidacloprid 17.8 SL spray karein

KATAI:
- Samay: October se November
- Jab 80% dane sone jaise rang ke ho jayein
- Combine harvester se kataai karein
- Jaldi kataai karein nahi toh dane girenge

=== MAIZE (MAKKA) - KHARIF/RABI DONO ===
================================================================

PARICHAY:
Makka teen mausam mein ho sakta hai - Kharif, Rabi aur Zaid. Yeh ek versatile crop hai jo animal feed, starch, oil aur food ke liye ugaya jata hai.

BONA KA SAMAY:
- Kharif: June se July (Monsoon ke saath)
- Rabi: October se November
- Zaid: February se March
- Beej dar: 20-25 kg per hectare
- Row distance: 60-75 cm, Plant distance: 20-25 cm

MITTI:
- Domat se bhari mitti (loamy to sandy loam)
- pH: 6.0 se 7.5
- Acchi drainage zaroori hai

KHAD:
- Nitrogen: 120-150 kg per hectare (3 kist mein)
- Phosphorus: 60 kg per hectare
- Potash: 40 kg per hectare
- Zinc: 25 kg per hectare

UNNAT KISAMEN:
- DKC-9144: Hybrid - zyada paidawar
- Pioneer 30V92: Kharif ke liye
- Vivek QPM 9: Protein rich
- HM-4: UP ke liye
- HQPM-1: Lysine rich

BIMARI:
1. Turcicum Leaf Blight: Patto par lamba bhoora daag
   Ilaj: Mancozeb spray karein
2. Stalk Rot: Tana gal jata hai
   Ilaj: Achha drainage rakhein, resistant variety lagaayein
3. Fall Armyworm: Naya keeda - patte kha jata hai
   Ilaj: Spinetoram 11.7 SC spray karein

PAIDAWAR: 50-70 quintal per hectare hybrid varieties se

=== TOMATO (TAMATAR) ===
================================================================

PARICHAY:
Tamatar ek important vegetable crop hai jo saal mein do baar ugaya ja sakta hai. Yeh commercial farming ke liye bahut profitable hai.

BONA KA SAMAY:
- Kharif: June-July
- Rabi: September-October
- Nursery mein pehle ugaayein - 25-30 din
- Transplanting: Jab paudha 15-20 cm ka ho

NURSERY TAIYARI:
- Raised beds banayein - 1m x 3m size
- Beej 1 cm gehraai mein lagayein
- Row to row: 10 cm, Beej to Beej: 5 cm
- Paani spray se dein - seedbed mat bigo
- 25-30 din mein transplant ke liye taiyar

KHET MEIN TRANSPLANTING:
- Row distance: 60-75 cm
- Plant distance: 45-60 cm
- Shaam ke waqt transplant karein
- Turant paani dein

KHAD:
- FYM (Gobar khad): 20-25 ton per hectare
- Nitrogen: 120 kg per hectare (4 kist mein)
- Phosphorus: 60 kg per hectare
- Potash: 60 kg per hectare
- Calcium Nitrate: 200 kg per hectare - fruit setting mein

SINCHAI:
- Drip irrigation sabse best hai
- Regular paani chahiye - sukha mat rehne do
- Fruit development mein zyada paani
- Pakne ke samay paani kam karein

SAHARA (STAKING):
- Bamboo ya lathi se support dein
- 1-1.5 meter ki lathi
- String se baandhein

UNNAT KISAMEN:
- Pusa Ruby: Desi variety
- Arka Vikas: IARI ki variety
- CO-3: Tamil Nadu ke liye
- Pusa Hybrid 1: Hybrid variety - zyada paidawar
- Rashmi: Maharashtra ke liye

BIMARI:
1. Early Blight: Patto par bhoore dabbe, circles mein
   Ilaj: Mancozeb 75 WP - 2 gram per liter spray
2. Late Blight: Patte aur fruit dono par
   Ilaj: Metalaxyl + Mancozeb spray karein
3. Fusarium Wilt: Paudha ek taraf jhuk ke sookh jata hai
   Ilaj: Soil drench Carbendazim se karein
4. Tomato Mosaic Virus: Patte mottled ho jaate hain
   Ilaj: Infected paudhe nikaalein, aphid control karein

KEEDE:
1. Fruit Borer: Fruit mein ghus jaata hai
   Ilaj: Spinosad 45 SC - 0.3 ml per liter spray
2. Whitefly: Chote safed keede - virus failaate hain
   Ilaj: Imidacloprid spray karein
3. Aphid (Maahu): Patto ke neeche chipke rehte hain
   Ilaj: Dimethoate spray karein

PAIDAWAR: 25-30 ton per hectare

=== PYAZ (ONION) ===
================================================================

PARICHAY:
Pyaz ek important vegetable aur commercial crop hai. India mein Maharashtra, Karnataka, Madhya Pradesh, Gujarat mein zyada hota hai.

BONA KA SAMAY:
- Kharif: June-July (Nursery May mein)
- Late Kharif: August-September
- Rabi: October-November (Nursery September mein)
- Rabi pyaz sabse zyada hota hai aur storage ke liye best hai

NURSERY:
- Raised beds mein ugayein
- Beej dar: 8-10 kg per hectare
- Mitti mein FYM acchi tarah milayein
- 6-8 hafte mein transplant ke liye taiyar

TRANSPLANTING:
- Row distance: 15 cm
- Plant distance: 10 cm
- Seedlings 15-20 cm ki honi chahiye
- Shaam ko transplant karein

KHAD:
- FYM: 20-25 ton per hectare
- Nitrogen: 100 kg per hectare (3 kist mein)
- Phosphorus: 50 kg per hectare
- Potash: 50 kg per hectare
- Sulphur: 20 kg per hectare - quality ke liye

SINCHAI:
- Har 7-10 din mein paani dein
- Bulb formation mein regular paani
- Harvest se 2-3 hafte pehle paani band karein - storage better hoga

UNNAT KISAMEN:
- Agrifound Dark Red: Popular variety
- Pusa Red: North India ke liye
- N-2-4-1: Maharashtra ke liye
- Arka Kalyan: IIHR Bangalore ki variety
- Agrifound White: Export ke liye

BIMARI:
1. Purple Blotch: Patto par purple dabbe
   Ilaj: Mancozeb ya Iprodione spray karein
2. Stemphylium Blight: Patte tip se sookhne lagte hain
   Ilaj: Chlorothalonil spray karein
3. Damping Off: Nursery mein paudhe gir jaate hain
   Ilaj: Captan se seed treatment karein

STORAGE:
- Harvest ke baad 2-3 din dhoop mein sukhayein
- Curing karein - 7-10 din airy jagah mein
- Proper ventilation wale store mein rakhein
- Temperature: 25-30 degree Celsius
- Humidity: 65-70%

PAIDAWAR: 25-30 ton per hectare Rabi mein

=== ALOO (POTATO) ===
================================================================

PARICHAY:
Aloo Rabi season ki sabse important vegetable crop hai. Yeh October-November mein lagaya jata hai aur February-March mein nikala jata hai.

BONA KA SAMAY:
- North India: October se November
- Hills: March se April
- Cut tubers lagayein - har piece mein 2-3 aankhein
- Seed dar: 20-25 quintal per hectare

KHET TAIYARI:
- Gehri jotai karein - 25-30 cm
- Achhi tarah barik mitti karein
- Ridge aur furrow system mein lagayein
- Row distance: 60 cm, Plant distance: 20-25 cm

KHAD:
- FYM: 20-25 ton per hectare
- Nitrogen: 120-150 kg per hectare (2 kist mein)
- Phosphorus: 80 kg per hectare
- Potash: 100-120 kg per hectare - yield quality ke liye important

SINCHAI:
- Bote ke turant baad paani dein
- Har 8-10 din mein regular sinchai
- Earthing up ke samay paani mat dein
- Harvest se 10-15 din pehle band karein

EARTHING UP:
- Bote ke 30-35 din baad mitti chadhayein
- Isse kand (tuber) dhak jaata hai - green nahi hota
- Doosri earthing up 50-60 din baad

UNNAT KISAMEN:
- Kufri Jyoti: Popular, late blight resistant
- Kufri Bahar: UP ke liye
- Kufri Sindhuri: Red skin - storage ke liye
- Kufri Chandramukhi: Early variety
- Kufri Pukhraj: High yielding

BIMARI:
1. Late Blight: Sabse dangerous - patte aur kand dono par
   Ilaj: Metalaxyl + Mancozeb spray karein turant
2. Early Blight: Patto par bhoore dabbe
   Ilaj: Mancozeb spray karein
3. Common Scab: Kand ki outer surface par kharcha pan
   Ilaj: pH 5.5-6.0 rakhein, zyada potash mat dein

PAIDAWAR: 200-250 quintal per hectare

=== SUGARCANE (GANNA) ===
================================================================

PARICHAY:
Ganna ek commercial crop hai jo sugar, jaggery (gur) aur bagasse ke liye ugaya jata hai. Yeh ek saal se zyada ka crop hai.

LAGANE KA SAMAY:
- Autumn (Sharad): October se November (Best)
- Spring (Basant): February se March
- Summer (Grishm): April ke antim mein
- Set distance: 60-90 cm row se row

KHET TAIYARI:
- Gehri jotai 30-35 cm
- 2-3 baar cross jotai
- Deep furrow banayein lagane ke liye

LAGANE KA TARIKA:
- 3 aankh (bud) wale tukde lagayein
- Seedling method ya set method
- Furrow mein flat ya slightly slant karke lagayein
- Mitti se 3-4 cm dhakein

KHAD:
- FYM: 25-30 ton per hectare
- Nitrogen: 250-300 kg per hectare (4 kist mein)
- Phosphorus: 60-80 kg per hectare
- Potash: 60-80 kg per hectare

SINCHAI:
- Lagane ke baad turant paani dein
- Germination mein: Har 7 din mein
- Growth period: Har 10-15 din mein
- Maturity mein: 20-25 din ke antral par
- Total: 8-10 sinchai

UNNAT KISAMEN:
- Co-0238: Sabse popular, high sucrose
- CoJ-64: North India ke liye
- Co-86032: Maharashtra ke liye
- CoS-8436: UP ke liye
- Co-0118: Early maturity

BIMARI:
1. Red Rot: Tane mein laal rang, khatta smell
   Ilaj: Resistant variety lagayein, hot water treatment
2. Smut (Kanda): Kaala whip nikalta hai
   Ilaj: Infected plants nikaalein, healthy seed lagayein
3. Wilt: Paudhe sookh jaate hain
   Ilaj: Proper drainage, Trichoderma soil application

PAIDAWAR: 70-80 ton per hectare

=== COTTON (KAPAS) ===
================================================================

PARICHAY:
Kapas India ki sabse important cash crop hai. Gujarat, Maharashtra, Telangana, Punjab mein zyada hoti hai. Isko "White Gold" bhi kehte hain.

BONA KA SAMAY:
- May ke antim se June tak
- Monsoon aane ke saath bona best
- Beej dar: 2.5-3 kg per hectare (hybrid)
- Row distance: 90-120 cm, Plant distance: 45-60 cm

MITTI:
- Kali mitti (black cotton soil) sabse best
- Deep, well-drained mitti
- pH: 6.0 se 8.0

KHAD:
- FYM: 10-12 ton per hectare
- Nitrogen: 120-150 kg per hectare (3 kist mein)
- Phosphorus: 60 kg per hectare
- Potash: 60 kg per hectare
- Boron: 1 kg per hectare - boll setting ke liye

SINCHAI:
- Jab rainfall na ho tab sinchai karein
- Critical stages: Boll formation aur boll development
- Drip irrigation best hai cotton ke liye

UNNAT KISAMEN:
- Bt Cotton Hybrids: Bollworm resistant
- RCH-2 Bt: Popular in Maharashtra
- MRC-7017: Gujarat ke liye
- NCS-855: Telangana ke liye
- Bunny Bt: Andhra Pradesh ke liye

BIMARI:
1. Bacterial Blight (Angular Leaf Spot):
   Ilaj: Streptomycin spray karein
2. Alternaria Leaf Spot:
   Ilaj: Mancozeb spray karein
3. Fusarium Wilt:
   Ilaj: Resistant variety, soil treatment

KEEDE:
1. Pink Bollworm: Boll ke andar keeda
   Ilaj: Bt variety lagayein, Profenophos spray
2. American Bollworm: Boll khaata hai
   Ilaj: NPV spray ya Spinosad
3. Whitefly: Virus failaata hai
   Ilaj: Imidacloprid spray
4. Aphid: Patto ka ras chosta hai
   Ilaj: Dimethoate spray

PAIDAWAR: 20-25 quintal (kapas) per hectare

=== GROUNDNUT (MOONGFALI) ===
================================================================

PARICHAY:
Moongfali ek important oilseed crop hai jo protein aur oil ke liye ugayi jati hai. Gujarat, Andhra Pradesh, Tamil Nadu, Karnataka mein zyada hoti hai.

BONA KA SAMAY:
- Kharif: June se July (Best season)
- Rabi: November se December (South India)
- Beej dar: 100-120 kg per hectare (shell)
- Row distance: 30-45 cm, Plant distance: 10-15 cm

MITTI:
- Balu mitti ya domat balu mitti (Sandy loam)
- Acchi drainage zaroori
- pH: 6.0 se 6.5
- Calcium rich mitti best hai

KHAD:
- FYM: 5 ton per hectare
- Nitrogen: 20-25 kg per hectare (Rhizobium se nitrogen fix hota hai)
- Phosphorus: 40-60 kg per hectare
- Potash: 40-60 kg per hectare
- Gypsum: 400-500 kg per hectare - peg penetration ke liye
- Boron: 1 kg per hectare

RHIZOBIUM INOCULATION:
- Beej lagane se pehle Rhizobium culture se coat karein
- Yeh nitrogen fix karta hai - khaad ki zaroorat kam hoti hai

SINCHAI:
- Bote ke baad ek paani zaroor
- Flowering mein paani zaroori
- Peg penetration (pegging) mein paani mat dein
- Pod filling mein regular paani

UNNAT KISAMEN:
- TAG-24: Popular, early maturity
- GG-20: Gujarat ke liye
- TMV-2: Tamil Nadu ke liye
- ICGS-11: Drought tolerant
- Kadiri-3: Andhra Pradesh ke liye

BIMARI:
1. Tikka (Leaf Spot): Early aur late - patto par dabbe
   Ilaj: Mancozeb ya Chlorothalonil spray
2. Collar Rot: Tane ka galna nursery mein
   Ilaj: Captan seed treatment, Bavistin soil drench
3. Stem Rot (White Mold): Safed fungal growth
   Ilaj: Carbendazim spray karein

PAIDAWAR: 20-25 quintal per hectare

================================================================

BONA KA SAMAY:
- September ke antim se October tak

- Beej dar: 5-6 kg per hectare (line mein)
- Row distance: 30-45 cm, Plant distance: 10-15 cm

MITTI:

- pH: 6.0 se 7.5
- Achhi drainage

KHAD:
- FYM: 10-15 ton per hectare
- Nitrogen: 80-100 kg per hectare (2 kist mein)
- Phosphorus: 40-50 kg per hectare
- Potash: 40 kg per hectare
- Sulphur: 30-40 kg per hectare (oil content badhata hai)
- Boron: 1 kg per hectare - pod setting ke liye important

SINCHAI:
- Sarson kum paani mein bhi ho jati hai
- 2-3 sinchai kaafi hain
- Pehli: Bote ke 25-30 din baad
- Doosri: Flowering mein
- Teesri: Pod filling mein (agar ho sake)

UNNAT KISAMEN:
- Pusa Bold: Sabse popular, bold seeds
- Pusa Mustard 25: New variety
- RH-30: Rajasthan ke liye
- Varuna: UP ke liye
- NRCHB-506: Bihar ke liye

BIMARI:
1. Alternaria Blight: Patto aur pods par bhoore dabbe
   Ilaj: Mancozeb 75 WP spray karein
2. Sclerotinia Stem Rot: Safed mycelium tane par
   Ilaj: Iprodione spray karein
3. White Rust: Safed pustules patto par
   Ilaj: Metalaxyl + Mancozeb spray

APHID (MAAHU):
- Sarson mein sabse bada keeda
- Jab temperature 15-20 degree ho tab zyada attack
- Ilaj: Imidacloprid 17.8 SL ya Dimethoate spray
- Timely spray zaroori hai - yield 20-30% kam ho sakti hai

PAIDAWAR: 15-20 quintal per hectare

================================================================
=== GENERAL FARMING TIPS ===
================================================================

MITTI PARIKSHAN (SOIL TESTING):
- Har 3 saal mein mitti test zaroor karwayein
- Soil Health Card se free testing hoti hai
- Test ke result ke hisaab se khaad dein
- Isse paisa bachta hai aur yield badhti hai

BEEJ UPCHAR (SEED TREATMENT):
- Fungicide se beej treat karein - Thiram ya Captan
- Rhizobium/PSB inoculant lagayein dal faslein mein
- Imidacloprid se treat karein soil borne keede ke liye
- Treatment ke baad seedbeds mein sheer sookhe

INTEGRATED PEST MANAGEMENT (IPM):
1. Fasal chakkar (Crop rotation) apnayein
2. Resistant varieties lagayein
3. Jaivik dawai pehle try karein
4. Chemical spray antim upaaya ho
5. Spray ka label dhyan se padein

JAIVIK KHETI (ORGANIC FARMING):
- FYM (gobar khad) zyada use karein
- Vermicompost banayein - kechua khad
- Neem khali (neem cake) use karein
- Jeevamrit banayein: 200L paani + 10kg gobar + 10kg gomutr + 2kg chane ka atta
- Panchgavya spray karein

PAANI KI BACHAT (WATER CONSERVATION):
- Drip irrigation lagayein - 40-50% paani bachta hai
- Mulching karein - mitti mein nami rahti hai
- Shaam ko paani dein - evaporation kam hoti hai
- Rain water harvesting karein

FASAL BIMA (CROP INSURANCE):
- PM Fasal Bima Yojana mein register karein
- Kharif: 2% premium farmer deta hai
- Rabi: 1.5% premium
- Natural aapda mein pura muavza milta hai

MANDI MEIN BECHNA:
- MSP (Minimum Support Price) check karein
- APMC mandi mein bechein
- e-NAM portal use karein online trading ke liye
- FPO (Farmer Producer Organization) join karein - better price milta hai
