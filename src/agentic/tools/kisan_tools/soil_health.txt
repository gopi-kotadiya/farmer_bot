=== MITTI KE PRAKAR (TYPES OF SOIL) ===
================================================================

1. KALI MITTI (BLACK SOIL / REGUR SOIL):
   - Kahan milti hai: Maharashtra, MP, Gujarat, Telangana, Karnataka
   - Khaasiyat: Paani rok ke rakhti hai, clay content zyada
   - Best crops: Cotton, Sugarcane, Wheat, Jowar, Sunflower
   - pH: 7.5 se 8.5 (slightly alkaline)
   - Calcium aur Magnesium zyada hota hai
   - Nitrogen aur Phosphorus ki kami hoti hai aksar

2. LAAL MITTI (RED SOIL):
   - Kahan milti hai: Tamil Nadu, Andhra Pradesh, Karnataka, Odisha
   - Khaasiyat: Iron oxide zyada - isliye laal rang
   - Best crops: Groundnut, Potato, Millets, Pulses
   - pH: 6.0 se 7.5
   - Nitrogen, Phosphorus, Humus ki kami hoti hai
   - Organic matter badhane ki zaroorat

3. DOMAT MITTI (LOAMY SOIL):
   - Kahan milti hai: Punjab, Haryana, UP, Bihar
   - Khaasiyat: Sand + Silt + Clay ka mix - sabse best mitti
   - Best crops: Wheat, Rice, Sugarcane, Vegetables - sab kuch
   - pH: 6.5 se 7.5
   - Sabse fertile mitti hai
   - Isme sab crops achhe se ho sakte hain

4. BALU MITTI (SANDY SOIL):
   - Kahan milti hai: Rajasthan, UP ke kuch hisse
   - Khaasiyat: Paani jaldi nikalti hai, hawa zyada
   - Best crops: Groundnut, Bajra, Watermelon, Moongfali
   - pH: 6.0 se 7.0
   - Organic matter bahut kam
   - Baar baar sinchai chahiye

5. CHIKNAI MITTI (CLAY SOIL):
   - Khaasiyat: Paani rok leti hai, jaldi package hoti hai
   - Best crops: Rice, Sugarcane
   - Drainage ki zaroorat
   - Gypsum aur organic matter milayein

6. PAHADI MITTI (HILL SOIL):
   - Kahan milti hai: Himachal, Uttarakhand, Northeast
   - Khaasiyat: Thin layer, acidic hoti hai
   - Best crops: Tea, Apple, Potato, Vegetables
   - pH: 4.5 se 6.0 (acidic)
   - Lime dene ki zaroorat aksar

7. ALLUVIAL MITTI (JALODHBHAV MITTI):
   - Kahan milti hai: Ganga plains, Brahmaputra valley
   - Khaasiyat: Nadion se ayi mitti - bahut fertile
   - Best crops: Rice, Wheat, Sugarcane, Vegetables sab
   - India ki 40% zameen par hai
   - Regularly flood se naveen hoti rehti hai

================================================================
=== MITTI KI pH (ACIDITY/ALKALINITY) ===
================================================================

pH SCALE SAMAJHNA:
- pH 0-6: Acidic (Khatti mitti)
- pH 7: Neutral (Sahi mitti)
- pH 8-14: Alkaline (Khari mitti)
- Best range crops ke liye: pH 6.0 se 7.5

pH KITNA HONA CHAHIYE FASAL KE HISAAB SE:
- Gehu: 6.0 - 7.5
- Dhan (Rice): 5.5 - 6.5
- Makka: 6.0 - 7.0
- Tomato: 6.0 - 7.0
- Pyaz: 6.0 - 7.0
- Aloo: 5.0 - 6.0
- Groundnut: 6.0 - 6.5
- Soybean: 6.0 - 7.0
- Cotton: 6.0 - 8.0
- Sugarcane: 6.0 - 7.5
- Tea: 4.5 - 5.5

ACIDIC MITTI THEEK KARNA:
- Lime (Calcium Carbonate): 2-4 ton per hectare
- Dolomite Lime: Magnesium bhi milata hai
- Wood ash: Chhoti quantity mein
- Kab dein: Bote se 2-4 hafte pehle
- Jitna acidic utna zyada lime

ALKALINE MITTI THEEK KARNA:
- Gypsum (Calcium Sulphate): 5-10 ton per hectare
- Sulphur powder: 200-400 kg per hectare
- FYM zyada dein - humus badhata hai pH kam karta hai
- Ammonium Sulphate fertilizer use karein
- Kab dein: Bote se 1-2 mahine pehle

=== MITTI KE POSHAN TATVA (SOIL NUTRIENTS) ===
================================================================

MUKHYA TATVA (MACRONUTRIENTS):

1. NITROGEN (NAIJAAN - N):
   - Kaam: Patto ka hara rang, badhwaar
   - Kami ke lakshan: Patte peele ho jaate hain, neeche ke pehle
   - Zyada hone par: Zyada patta, kam phal/dana
   - Strot: Urea (46% N), DAP (18% N), Ammonium Sulphate (21% N)
   - Dene ka samay: 2-3 kist mein dein - ek baar mat dein
   - Khaas baat: Pani mein ghul ke neeche jata hai - chhota chhota dein

2. PHOSPHORUS (PHOSPHORUS - P):
   - Kaam: Jad (roots) ki badhwaar, phool aur phal banana
   - Kami ke lakshan: Patte neele-hare ya purple ho jaate hain
   - Strot: DAP (46% P2O5), SSP (16% P2O5), Rock Phosphate
   - Dene ka samay: Bote ke samay poora dein - khet mein mila dein
   - Khaas baat: Mitti mein zyada samay rahta hai - ek baar mein dein

3. POTASSIUM (POTASH - K):
   - Kaam: Fruit quality, disease resistance, paani regulation
   - Kami ke lakshan: Patte ke kinare jal jaate hain, brown ho jaate hain
   - Strot: MOP/KCl (60% K2O), SOP (50% K2O)
   - Dene ka samay: Bote ke samay poora ya 2 kist mein
   - Khaas baat: Fruit aur vegetable crops mein zyada zaroori

MADHYAM TATVA (SECONDARY NUTRIENTS):

4. CALCIUM (Ca):
   - Kaam: Cell wall banana, root growth
   - Kami ke lakshan: Naye patte moda hue, tip burn
   - Strot: Lime, Gypsum, Calcium Nitrate
   - Tomato, Groundnut mein zyada zaroori

5. MAGNESIUM (Mg):
   - Kaam: Chlorophyll banana (hara rang)
   - Kami ke lakshan: Purane patte ke beech peele pad jaate hain
   - Strot: Dolomite lime, Magnesium Sulphate
   - Dene ka samay: Dolomite bote se pehle mitti mein

6. SULPHUR (S):
   - Kaam: Protein banana, oil content
   - Kami ke lakshan: Naye patte peele, uniform yellowing
   - Strot: Gypsum, Ammonium Sulphate, Elemental Sulphur
   - Sarson, Onion, Garlic mein zyada zaroori

SUKSHAM TATVA (MICRONUTRIENTS):

7. ZINC (JASTA - Zn):
   - Sabse common kami India mein
   - Kami ke lakshan: Naye patte chote, stippled, brown spots
   - Ilaj: Zinc Sulphate 25 kg per hectare mitti mein
   - Ya: 0.5% ZnSO4 spray karein (5 gram per liter)
   - Kab dein: Bote se pehle ya pehli sinchai baad

8. IRON (LOHA - Fe):
   - Kami ke lakshan: Naye patte peele, naso ke beech
   - Zyada alkaline mitti mein kami hoti hai
   - Ilaj: FeSO4 0.5% spray karein
   - Chelated iron use karein alkaline mitti mein

9. BORON (B):
   - Kaam: Phool aur phal banana, cell division
   - Kami ke lakshan: Terminal bud marna, hollow stem
   - Ilaj: Borax 1-2 kg per hectare mitti mein
   - Ya: 0.2% Boric acid spray
   - Cauliflower, Mustard, Sunflower mein zaroori

10. MANGANESE (Mn):
    - Kami ke lakshan: Naye patto mein interveinal chlorosis
    - Ilaj: MnSO4 spray ya mitti mein milayein

11. COPPER (TAAMBA - Cu):
    - Kami: Patte muda hue, bluish green rang
    - Ilaj: CuSO4 2.5 kg per hectare

12. MOLYBDENUM (Mo):
    - Dal faslon mein zaroori - Rhizobium ke liye
    - Kami: Patte cupped, twisted

=== MITTI PARIKSHAN (SOIL TESTING) ===
================================================================

KYUN ZAROORI HAI:
- Sahi khaad ka pata chalat hai - paisa bachta hai
- Zyada khaad dene se nuksan hota hai
- Mitti ki pH pata chalti hai
- Soil Health Card scheme mein free hota hai

KAISE KARWAYEIN:
- Nazdiki Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK) mein jayen
- Soil Health Card portal par: soilhealth.dac.gov.in
- Private labs mein bhi hoti hai - Rs 200-500 mein
- Government ke through bilkul free

SAMPLE LENE KA TARIKA:
1. Khet ke 8-10 jagah se mitti lo
2. Upar ki 15-20 cm mitti lo (plow layer)
3. Sab mix karo ek jagah
4. Pathri, roots, grass nikalo
5. 500 gram sample lab mein do
6. Khali khet se lo - fasal hone par mat lo

REPORT MEIN KYA HOTA HAI:
- pH value
- Organic Carbon (%)
- Available N, P, K
- Secondary: S, Ca, Mg
- Micronutrients: Zn, Fe, Mn, Cu, B

=== ORGANIC MATTER AUR HUMUS ===
================================================================

ORGANIC MATTER KYUN ZAROORI:
- Mitti ki structure sudhaarta hai
- Paani rokne ki shakti badhta hai
- Nutrients dhire dhire milate hain
- Mitti ke keede zinda rehte hain - beneficial
- pH balance rehta hai

ORGANIC MATTER KAISE BADHAYEIN:

1. FYM (GOBAR KHAD):
   - Paka hua gobar - 6 mahine purana
   - 10-15 ton per hectare per year
   - Bote se 3-4 hafte pehle mitti mein milayein
   - NPK ratio: 0.5:0.25:0.5 approximately

2. VERMICOMPOST (KECHUA KHAD):
   - Kechua se bana organic khad
   - 4-5 ton per hectare kaafi hai
   - FYM se 3-4 guna zyada assardar
   - Khud bana sakte ho ghar mein

3. GREEN MANURE (HARA KHAD):
   - Dhaincha, Sunhemp, Cowpea ugao
   - 45-60 din mein jotai kar ke mitti mein milao
   - 50-60 kg Nitrogen per hectare milta hai
   - Free khad hai!

4. CROP RESIDUE:
   - Fasal ke baad thaan mat jalao
   - Mitti mein joto - decompose hoga
   - Carbon organic matter badhata hai

5. NEEM CAKE (NEEM KHALI):
   - 200-400 kg per hectare mitti mein milao
   - Nitrogen milta hai + nematode control
   - Soil borne insects bhi kam hote hain

=== MITTI KI SEHAT SUDHARANA (SOIL IMPROVEMENT) ===
================================================================

BALI MITTI KO BEHTAR BANANA:
- FYM zyada dein - 20-25 ton
- Clay soil thodi mila sakte hain
- Mulching karein - nami rokne ke liye
- Drip irrigation lagayein
- Organic matter badhao har saal

CHIKNAI (CLAY) MITTI KO BEHTAR BANANA:
- Gypsum 5-10 ton per hectare dein
- FYM dein - structure badhta hai
- Raised bed farming karein
- Deep plowing karein
- Sandy material mix karein

USAD (SALINE) MITTI:
- Pehchaan: Upar safed namak ki parat
- Gypsum dein - 10-12 ton per hectare
- Zyada paani dein - leaching ke liye
- Salt tolerant varieties lagayen
- Dhaan (Rice) ugana - leaching mein madad

KHAR (ALKALINE) MITTI:
- Sulphur powder dein - 400-600 kg per hectare
- Gypsum dein
- FYM zyada dein
- Organic matter badhao
- Ammonium sulphate use karein Urea ki jagah

ACIDIC MITTI (KHATTI):
- Lime dein - Agricultural lime ya Dolomite
- 2-4 ton per hectare
- Bote se 1 mahine pehle dein
- Baar baar test karwate raho

=== JAIVIK KHETI (ORGANIC FARMING) ===
================================================================

JEEVAMRIT BANANE KA TARIKA:
- 200 liter paani
- 10 kg desi gaay ka taza gobar
- 10 liter gomutr (cow urine)
- 2 kg chane ka atta ya besan
- 500 gram gudd ya ganna ka ras
- 1 muthi khet ki mitti
Sab mix karein - 3-7 din chhodein ferment hone do
Har 3-4 din stirring karein
Dose: 200 liter per acre per spray

PANCHGAVYA BANANA:
- 3 kg gobar
- 2 liter gomutr
- 2 liter doodh
- 1 kg dahi
- 1 liter ghee
Mix karein - 7 din ferment karein
Dose: 3% solution bana ke spray karein

DASHPARNI ARK (KEEDO KE LIYE):
- 10 alag alag tarah ke patte
  (Neem, Dhatura, Papaya, Aak, Tulsi, Nirgundi, Custard apple, 
   Pomegranate, Castor, Bael)
- 10 liter paani mein ubaalein
- Thanda karein - filter karein
- 3-5% solution bana ke spray karein
- Keede aur bimari dono ke liye

NEEM BASED SPRAY:
- Neem patti ras: 10 kg patti pees ke 200 liter paani mein
- Ya Neem tel (oil): 5 ml per liter paani
- Ya Neem cake extract: 100 gram per liter bhigo ke
- Har 10-15 din mein spray

=== MITTI CONSERVATION (MITTI BACHANA) ===
================================================================

EROSION SE BACHAV:
- Cover crops lagayein - mitti dhakein
- Contour farming karein pahad par
- Windbreaks lagayein - hawa se erosion rokein
- Minimum tillage karein jahan ho sake
- Mulching karein - plastic ya organic

WATER CONSERVATION:
- Bund banayein khet mein - paani rokein
- Check dam banayein - khet ke paas
- Farm pond banayein - baarish ka paani store karein
- Mulching se 40-50% paani bachta hai
- Drip irrigation se 60% paani bachta hai

CROP ROTATION KE FAYDE:
- Dal fasal baad anaj fasal - nitrogen badhta hai
- Alag roots - alag depth tak poshan nikalte hain
- Ek hi fasal baar baar - ek hi poshan kam hota hai
- Pest aur disease cycle toot ta hai
- Mitti healthy rehti hai

GOOD CROP ROTATION:
- Gehu → Moong → Gehu (North India)
- Rice → Wheat → Moong (Punjab, Haryana)
- Cotton → Wheat → Moong (Gujarat)
- Sugarcane → Wheat → Moong (UP)
