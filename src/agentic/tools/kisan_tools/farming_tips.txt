=== MAUSAM KE HISAAB SE KHETI (SEASONAL FARMING) ===
================================================================

KHARIF SEASON (JUNE - NOVEMBER):
- Monsoon season ki fasal
- Bona: June-July (Monsoon ke saath)
- Katai: September-November
- Main crops: Dhan, Makka, Cotton, Sugarcane, Groundnut, Soybean,
             Arhar, Moong, Urad, Bajra, Jowar
- Tips:
  * Baarish shuru hone se pehle khet taiyar rakhein
  * Drainage ka intezam zaroor karein
  * Seed treatment zaroor karein fungal disease se bachne ke liye
  * Weed control - pehle 30-40 din bahut zaroori

RABI SEASON (NOVEMBER - APRIL):
- Winter season ki fasal
- Bona: October-December
- Katai: March-April
- Main crops: Gehu, Sarson, Chana, Matar, Masoor, Jau, Potato
- Tips:
  * Timely sowing bahut zaroori - der hone par yield kam
  * Frost se bachav zaroori hai kuch crops mein
  * Irrigation schedule regular rakhein
  * Aphid attack ka dhyan rakhein

ZAID SEASON (MARCH - JUNE):
- Summer season ki fasal
- Main crops: Watermelon, Cucumber, Pumpkin, Moong, Fodder crops
- Tips:
  * Garmi se bachav ke liye mulching zaroor karein
  * Zyada paani chahiye - sinchai frequent rakhein
  * Shade net use karein extreme heat mein

=== BEEJ CHAYAN AUR UPCHAR (SEED SELECTION & TREATMENT) ===
================================================================

ACHHA BEEJ KAISE CHUNEIN:
1. Certified seed hi khardein - government approved
2. Namdhari, Pioneer, Bayer, Syngenta - reputed companies
3. KVK se free seed bhi milti hai aksar
4. Label check karein: Germination %, Purity %, Expiry date
5. Theli phati na ho, alag rang ke daane na hon
6. Khud ke khet ka beej rakhein - 3-4 saal tak kaam karta hai
7. Hybrid varieties ka beej second year use mat karein

BEEJ UPCHAR (SEED TREATMENT):

Fungicide Treatment:
- Captan 75 WP: 2-3 gram per kg beej
- Thiram 75 WS: 2-3 gram per kg beej
- Carboxin 37.5% + Thiram 37.5%: 3 gram per kg beej
- Tarika: Thodi si paani mein paste banao, beej par lagao, sukha lo

Bio-inoculants:
- Rhizobium (Dal crops ke liye): 200 gram per 10 kg beej
- Azospirillum (Anaj crops ke liye): 200 gram per 10 kg beej
- PSB (Phosphate Solubilizing Bacteria): 200 gram per 10 kg beej
- Trichoderma viride: 4 gram per kg beej (soil borne disease ke liye)
- Tarika: Gur ya rice kanji mein mix karke beej par coat karein

Insecticide Treatment:
- Imidacloprid 70 WS: 7-10 gram per kg beej (sucking insects ke liye)
- Thiamethoxam 30 FS: 10 ml per kg beej

NOTE: Pehle fungicide, phir bio-inoculant - ek saath mat lagao!

=== KHET KI TAIYARI (LAND PREPARATION) ===
================================================================

JOTAI KE TYPES:
1. Deep Plowing (Gehri Jotai):
   - Mitti palat ke haal se karo
   - 25-30 cm gehraai
   - Garmi mein karo - keede marein, weeds sookhein
   - Har 3-4 saal mein ek baar

2. Secondary Tillage:
   - Rotavator ya cultivator se karo
   - Mitti barik karo
   - 2-3 baar cross direction mein

3. Bed aur Furrow System:
   - Ridge-furrow mein lagao
   - Irrigation aur drainage dono behtar
   - Potatoes, Sugarcane ke liye best

4. Zero Tillage:
   - Directly previous crop ke stubble mein bona
   - Gehu ke liye Happy Seeder use karein
   - Mitti nahi jotni - time aur paisa bachta hai
   - Soil structure maintain rehta hai

LEVELING (SAMATLANA):
- Laser leveling best hai - ek baar mein sab sahi
- Paani ek samaan milta hai
- 20-25% paani bachta hai
- Yield 5-10% badhti hai
- Ek baar ka kharcha - kai saal kaam karta hai

=== KHAAD KA SAHI UPYOG (FERTILIZER MANAGEMENT) ===
================================================================

4R NUTRITION:
1. Right Source: Sahi khaad chunein
2. Right Rate: Sahi matra mein dein
3. Right Time: Sahi samay par dein
4. Right Place: Sahi jagah dein

BASAL DOSE (BOTE KE SAMAY):
- Pura Phosphorus (P) - bote ke samay
- Pura Potash (K) - bote ke samay
- 1/3 Nitrogen - bote ke samay

TOP DRESSING:
- 2/3 Nitrogen - 2 kist mein bonate waqt baad
- Urea broadcasting: Subah ya shaam, nami ho mitti mein
- Fertigation (drip ke through): Zyada efficient

KHAD KHARIDNE KE TIPS:
- Licensed dealer se khardein
- Bill zaroor lo
- DAP aur Urea par subsidy milti hai
- Naqli khaad se bachein - Soil Health Card se verify karein
- Mixture khad kam efficient - single khad better

MICRO NUTRIENTS:
- Zinc: Sabse zyada kami - Zinc Sulphate 25 kg per hectare
- Boron: Flowers aur fruits ke liye - 1-2 kg per hectare
- Iron: Alkaline mitti mein - Ferrous Sulphate spray
- Magnesium: Dolomite lime se milta hai

FOLIAR SPRAY (PATTO PAR SPRAY):
- Kab karein: Jab jaldi absorption chahiye
- 19:19:19 (Water Soluble Fertilizer): 5 gram per liter
- 0:52:34: Flowering ke samay - fruits set karne ke liye
- Magnesium Sulphate: 10 gram per liter
- Zinc Sulphate: 5 gram per liter

=== SINCHAI KA SAHI TARIKA (IRRIGATION MANAGEMENT) ===
================================================================

SINCHAI KE METHODS:

1. FLOOD IRRIGATION (BORING/CANAL):
   - Sabse purani method
   - Paani zyada lagta hai - 60-70% waste
   - Leveling acchi zaroor karo

2. FURROW IRRIGATION:
   - Naali mein paani chalao
   - Flood se 30% paani bachta hai
   - Row crops ke liye best

3. SPRINKLER IRRIGATION:
   - Hawa se paani udaya jaata hai
   - 30-40% paani ki bachat
   - Wheat, Vegetables ke liye
   - Cost: Rs 40,000-60,000 per acre

4. DRIP IRRIGATION (SABSE BEST):
   - Seedha jad ke paas paani deta hai
   - 50-70% paani ki bachat
   - Fertilizer bhi paani mein de sakte hain (Fertigation)
   - Fruits, Vegetables, Sugarcane ke liye
   - Government subsidy: 50-90% milti hai
   - Cost: Rs 60,000-80,000 per acre

CRITICAL IRRIGATION STAGES:
- Gehu: Crown Root Initiation (CRI), Tillering, Jointing, Flowering, Grain Filling
- Dhan: Transplanting ke baad, Panicle Initiation, Flowering
- Makka: Knee High stage, Tasseling, Grain Filling
- Cotton: Squaring, Boll Formation, Boll Development

PAANI KI JAROORAT (WATER REQUIREMENT):
- Dhan: 1200-1500 mm (sabse zyada)
- Sugarcane: 1200-1500 mm
- Wheat: 400-500 mm
- Maize: 500-700 mm
- Cotton: 700-1000 mm
- Groundnut: 500-700 mm

=== WEED CONTROL (KHARPATWAR NIYANTRAN) ===
================================================================

WEEDS SE NUKSAN:
- Nutrients chheen te hain - 30-40% yield kam
- Paani chheen te hain
- Keede aur bimari ka ghar bante hain
- Katai mushkil ho jaati hai

WEED CONTROL KE TARIKE:

1. MANUAL WEEDING:
   - Hath ya khurpa se nikalo
   - 20-25 din aur 40-45 din par karo
   - Mehnat zyada - lekin safe hai

2. MECHANICAL WEEDING:
   - Wheel hoe ya cultivator se
   - Row crops mein achha kaam karta hai

3. HERBICIDES (KHARPATWAR NASH DAWAYI):

   Pre-emergence (Bote ke baad, ugne se pehle):
   - Pendimethalin 30 EC: 3-3.5 liter per hectare
   - Atrazine 50 WP: 1-1.5 kg per hectare (Maize)
   - Spray: Bote ke 0-3 din ke andar, nami ho mitti mein

   Post-emergence (Fasal ugne ke baad):
   - 2,4-D: 1 kg per hectare (Wheat mein broad leaf weeds)
   - Metsulfuron: 4 gram per hectare (Wheat)
   - Quizalofop: Grass weeds ke liye
   - Spray: 20-35 din baad jab weed 2-4 leaf stage mein ho

IMPORTANT TIPS:
- Nozzle flat fan ya flood jet use karein - herbicide ke liye
- Hawa nahi chalti ho tab spray karein
- Adjacent crop ke paas drift mat hone dein
- Gloves aur mask zaroor pahno

=== FASAL CHAKKAR (CROP ROTATION) ===
================================================================

FASAL CHAKKAR KYUN ZAROORI:
- Ek hi fasal se mitti ki ek nutrient ki kami hoti hai
- Pest aur disease ka cycle toot ta hai
- Income regular milti hai - ek fasal kharab to doosri bach jati hai
- Organic matter balance rehta hai

ACHHE FASAL CHAKKAR:

NORTH INDIA (Punjab, Haryana, UP):
- Rice - Wheat - Moong (3 fasal)
- Potato - Wheat - Paddy
- Cotton - Wheat (2 fasal)
- Sugarcane - Wheat - Moong

CENTRAL INDIA (MP, Rajasthan):
- Soybean - Wheat (achha)
- Jowar - Wheat
- Cotton - Gram (chana)

SOUTH INDIA:
- Rice - Groundnut
- Cotton - Jowar
- Sugarcane - Vegetables

TIPS:
- Dal (pulse) fasal ke baad anaj fasal lagao - nitrogen milta hai
- Deep rooted fasal ke baad shallow rooted lagao
- Ek hi family ki fasal baar baar mat lagao

=== MULCHING (MITTI DHAKNA) ===
================================================================

MULCHING KE FAYDE:
- Mitti mein nami rokta hai
- Weeds kam ho jaate hain
- Mitti ka temperature stable rehta hai
- Soil erosion rokta hai
- Organic matter badhta hai (organic mulch)

MULCHING KE TYPES:

1. PLASTIC MULCH:
   - Black ya silver plastic sheet
   - Sabse effective - weed aur moisture dono control
   - Vegetables, Strawberry ke liye
   - Government subsidy milti hai

2. ORGANIC MULCH:
   - Straw (paudhe ki thaan)
   - Sugarcane leaves (tops)
   - Dry leaves
   - 3-4 inch mota bichho
   - Free hai - khet ka hi material use karo

3. LIVE MULCH:
   - Intercrop lagao - Moong, Cowpea vegetables ke saath
   - Mitti dhakti hai + nitrogen bhi milta hai

=== MODERN FARMING TECHNIQUES ===
================================================================

1. PROTECTED CULTIVATION (GREENHOUSE):
   - Polyhouse ya shade net mein kheti
   - Off-season vegetables - zyada daam milta hai
   - Subsidy: 50% tak government deti hai
   - Best crops: Capsicum, Cucumber, Tomato

2. HYDROPONICS:
   - Mitti nahi - sirf paani aur nutrients
   - Less space, year round production
   - High value crops: Lettuce, Herbs
   - Investment zyada - return bhi zyada

3. SYSTEM OF RICE INTENSIFICATION (SRI):
   - Kam beej, zyada space, zyada yield
   - 8-10 din ki seedling use karein
   - 25x25 cm spacing
   - Paani ruk ruk ke dein (alternate wetting drying)
   - 15-20% zyada yield, 30% kam paani

4. DIRECT SEEDED RICE (DSR):
   - Nursery nahi - seedha khet mein bona
   - Labour aur paani dono bachte hain
   - Herbicide management zaroori

5. NATURAL FARMING (SUBHASH PALEKAR):
   - Zero budget kheti
   - Desi gaay aadhar hai - gobar aur gomutr
   - Jeevamrit, Bijamrit, Acchadana (mulching), Waaphasa

6. PRECISION FARMING:
   - GPS aur sensors ka use
   - Exact jagah par fertilizer aur water
   - Drone se spray - time aur dawa dono bachte hain
   - KVK se sikho - workshops hoti hain
